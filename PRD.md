Project Requirements Document (PRD)
Project Name

OneStop AI – Personalized Career & Education Advisor

1. Project Overview
Problem Statement

Students often struggle to make informed decisions about their education and careers. They rely on scattered sources such as YouTube videos, blogs, coaching institutes, and social media, leading to confusion and inconsistent guidance. There is no single platform that provides personalized recommendations based on a student's interests, academic background, skills, and career goals.

This project aims to solve that problem by offering an AI-powered platform that guides students throughout their educational and career journey.

2. Project Vision

To build an intelligent platform that helps every student discover the right career path, learn the required skills, explore educational opportunities, and prepare for future jobs using personalized AI recommendations.

3. Mission

Provide students with accurate, personalized, and actionable career guidance through one integrated platform instead of requiring multiple websites and applications.

4. Objectives
Help students identify suitable careers.
Recommend educational paths based on interests and goals.
Create personalized learning roadmaps.
Recommend certifications and courses.
Assist with resume improvement.
Track learning progress.
Prepare students for internships and placements.
